# Problem Statement

Indian cities experience recurring congestion around office corridors, metro stations, markets, colleges, stadiums, and event venues. Commuters may discover accidents, waterlogging, diversions, and overcrowding only after entering an affected route.

City teams also need a unified operational view that connects road congestion with pedestrian and public-transit crowding.
