
## Purpose

Define the primary users of TrafficPulse India,
their problems, needs, and the value provided by the platform.

TrafficPulse has four target user groups:

1. Commuters
2. Traffic-control / city operators
3. Transit operators
4. Urban planners

---

# 1. Commuters

## Who are they?

People travelling through Indian cities for:

- Work
- College
- Shopping
- Events
- Daily activities
- Inter-city travel

## Problems

- Unexpected congestion
- Accidents discovered too late
- Waterlogging
- Road closures
- Crowded transit hubs
- Event-related traffic
- Uncertainty about route conditions

## What they need

- Current mobility information
- Alternative routes
- ETA
- Delay warnings
- Crowd alerts
- Incident explanations
- Route recommendations

## TrafficPulse value

TrafficPulse helps commuters understand:

**What is happening → Why → Which route to take**

---

# 2. Traffic-Control / City Operators

## Who are they?

Personnel responsible for monitoring
and responding to urban mobility conditions.

Examples:

- Traffic-control teams
- City mobility teams
- Smart-city operations teams
- Emergency coordination teams

## Problems

- Fragmented information sources
- Multiple incidents happening simultaneously
- Difficulty identifying emerging hotspots
- Limited unified mobility view
- Slow understanding of incident impact
- Difficulty prioritizing areas

## What they need

- City-wide mobility overview
- Incident monitoring
- Hotspot detection
- Traffic trends
- Crowd trends
- Weather-related risk
- Evidence and confidence
- Affected-network analysis

## TrafficPulse value

TrafficPulse converts scattered mobility signals
into an operational intelligence layer.

---

# 3. Transit Operators

## Who are they?

Organizations managing public transportation systems.

Examples:

- Metro operators
- Bus operators
- Public-transit authorities

## Problems

- Crowd surges
- Overcrowded stations
- Disruptions near stations
- Event-driven passenger increases
- Traffic affecting access to transit hubs

## What they need

- Station crowd trends
- Passenger-demand context
- Nearby road congestion
- Incident awareness
- Event awareness
- Mobility hotspot detection

## TrafficPulse value

TrafficPulse connects transit conditions with
the surrounding road and pedestrian environment.

---

# 4. Urban Planners

## Who are they?

People involved in longer-term transportation
and infrastructure planning.

Examples:

- Urban planners
- Mobility planners
- Transport researchers
- Infrastructure teams

## Problems

- Recurring congestion
- Repeated incident locations
- Transit pressure
- Crowd concentration
- Infrastructure bottlenecks

## What they need

- Historical mobility patterns
- Recurring hotspots
- Accident patterns
- Transit demand
- Event impact
- Geographic mobility analysis

## TrafficPulse value

TrafficPulse can provide aggregated mobility
patterns that support longer-term planning.

---

# 8. Hackathon Focus

The MVP should primarily demonstrate value
for two groups:

### Commuter

Can TrafficPulse recommend a better route
when mobility conditions change?

### City Operator

Can TrafficPulse detect and explain an
emerging mobility hotspot?

These two experiences form the main
hackathon product story.

---

# 9. Long-Term Vision

TrafficPulse India should evolve into a shared
mobility intelligence layer serving:

- Citizens
- City authorities
- Transit systems
- Urban planners

The core intelligence system remains shared,
while each user receives a different interface
and decision output.
